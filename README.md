## 🎮 About the Game

ELDEN RING is an open-world action role-playing game developed by FromSoftware and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment. It launched on February 25, 2022. The Tarnished Pack adds new starting classes, equipment, weapons, and Torrent appearance customizations to the Lands Between.

---

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **ELDEN RING Tarnished Pack**. This trainer provides optional gameplay assistance for exploration, character progression, inventory management, and combat. It is designed around real ELDEN RING systems such as health, FP, stamina, runes, equipment durability, item quantities, and character attributes.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with character builds, explore the Lands Between with fewer restrictions, test Tarnished Pack equipment, or reduce repetitive progression while playing offline.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer after loading into the game world. Movement itself remains controlled by the game, including walking, sprinting, crouching, jumping, fast travel, and mounted travel with Torrent. Infinite stamina can make extended sprinting and traversal easier, while movement-related options should be enabled only when needed.

### Tools & Inventory

Open the inventory before editing item quantities or enabling unlimited consumables. Select the desired item, apply the change, and confirm that the quantity is displayed correctly. Avoid editing key items, quest items, unique equipment, or unknown entries. Save the game normally after verifying changes.

### World & Environment

The trainer can assist with exploration across the Lands Between and the Realm of Shadow by preserving health, FP, stamina, runes, and selected resources. Tarnished Pack equipment and Torrent appearance customizations remain subject to the game's normal unlock and equipment systems unless specifically supported by the trainer.

### Combat & Weapons

Enable combat options before entering an encounter. Infinite health, FP, and stamina support sustained attacks, guarding, dodging, weapon skills, sorceries, incantations, and spirit summons. Disable options before interacting with NPCs, entering loading screens, or connecting to online services.
